Archive note

1996

Two tides a day, one wardrobe.

Lowtide is the hour the river gives things back.

It started on the foreshore below Wapping, where the Thames drops seven metres twice a day and leaves the riverbed open to anyone with boots and a torch. Everyone down there had somewhere else to be by the time the water came back. The clothes had to work for both.

That is still the brief. Heavy enough to hold its shape through a night out, plain enough that the only loud thing about it is the fact you own one.
