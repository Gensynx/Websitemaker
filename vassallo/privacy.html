Cookies and the configurator

Our website sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Our fonts and images are served from our own site, so a visit does not send your details to font or advertising companies. Cloudflare may use a strictly necessary security cookie to tell people apart from automated attacks.

The door and window configurator runs in your browser. To save you starting again, it stores your latest design in your browser's local storage, and which panels you had open for as long as the tab stays open. That information stays on your device and is never sent to us unless you choose to send us your design. You can clear it at any time in your browser's settings.

Your rights

You can ask us, free of charge, to do any of the following. Email info@vassallodevelopments.com and we will reply within one month.

  • See a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your information, where we no longer need it or have no other lawful reason to keep it
  • Limit how we use it while a concern is being looked into
  • Object to a use based on our legitimate interests
  • Receive information you gave us in a format you can pass to someone else
  • Withdraw consent you gave, without affecting what we did before

Some rights have limits. For example, we must keep tax records even if you ask us to delete them, and we will explain if that applies.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our services change, for example when we start taking deposits online. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
