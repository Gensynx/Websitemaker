Services & fees

Seven services. One fixed fee each.

Everything a small business or self-employed person needs from an accountant, from one team. Every fee is fixed and agreed in writing before work starts.

Book a free consultation Jump to fees

01 Services

What we do

Open any service for what is included, how it runs step by step and the deadlines that apply.

  1. 01

    Bookkeeping

    Daily transactions, bank reconciliation and clean, up-to-date books on Sage or QuickBooks, so you always know where you stand.

  2. 02

    Payroll

    PAYE registration, payslips, RTI submissions and pension auto-enrolment. Your team paid correctly and on time, every time.

  3. 03

    Self Assessment & Tax Returns

    Personal tax returns for the self-employed, landlords, investors and directors. Filed accurately, on time, and never paying more than you owe.

  4. 04

    VAT Returns

    VAT registration, the right scheme for your business and accurate quarterly returns, fully Making Tax Digital compliant.

  5. 05

    Annual Accounts

    Year-end statutory accounts and corporation tax returns, filed with Companies House and HMRC, with the numbers explained in plain English.

  6. 06

    Company Formation & Secretarial

    Start your limited company properly: incorporation, share structure, registered office and every Companies House form thereafter.

  7. 07

    Tax Planning & Investigations

    Proactive year-round tax planning, cash flow forecasts and calm, expert support if HMRC ever comes calling.

Every engagement is a fixed fee agreed in writing before work starts, based on the services you need and the state of your records. Starting prices for each service are in the fees table. The consultation that produces your quote is free.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. With your permission we write to your current accountant for professional clearance and your records, and handle the whole handover.
