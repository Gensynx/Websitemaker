Archive note

1989

Two postcodes, one wardrobe.

Nuisance was what they called us before it was what we called ourselves.

It started in a gym off Ladbroke Grove — the kind with a ring in the middle, a board on the ropes and no mirrors. Everyone in there had somewhere else to be by ten. The clothes had to work for both.

That is still the brief. Heavy enough to hold its shape through a night out, plain enough that the only loud thing about it is the fact you own one.
