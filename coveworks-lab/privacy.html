06 Privacy

What we do with your details.

The short version: we collect only what you choose to send us, we use it to reply to you, and we do not track you around the site.

Who we are

This site is run by Coveworks Ltd, an independent web studio. We are the controller of the personal information described here. For anything about your information, write to sales@coveworks.tech.

What we collect, and when

When you send us a build or a message. Your name, your email address, your phone number if you give one, what you wrote, and the build you made in the configurator: the kind of site, the look, the sections, the headline and the words you typed to describe it. If you pasted the address of your current website, we keep that address too.

When you paste a web address into the configurator. Our server opens that public page once and reads a few details from it: the business name, the main headline, the description, the main colour and the logo. A temporary copy of those details is kept so a repeat lookup is quick, and is cleared automatically. Nothing else is kept unless you then send us the build.

To stop misuse. When you send a form or look up a site, we keep a scrambled record of your internet address for a short time, so that nobody can flood the forms. It is cleared automatically, normally within hours.

What we do not do. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Describing a site in words is read in your own browser and sends us nothing until you press send. Like any website, the company that hosts ours keeps standard server logs.

Why we use it

To reply to you, talk through your project and prepare a quote, which are steps you have asked us to take. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we never sell or share your details for marketing.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Enquiries are held on our web hosting and in our email, both provided by IONOS. We keep an enquiry for up to twelve months after we last spoke, then delete it. If you become a client, we keep what we need for the work and for our business records.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email sales@coveworks.tech and we will reply within a month. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
