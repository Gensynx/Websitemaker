Our story

Founded in the City. Working across London.

Fenwick & Rowe was founded in 2012 as an independent firm of certified accountants with a simple aim: clear numbers, fixed fees and an accountant who picks up the phone, for every client, whatever their size.

From our office at 4 Larch Yard in the City of London we serve sole traders, landlords, contractors and limited companies across City of London, Shoreditch, Clerkenwell, Islington, Hackney, Southwark and the wider London area. Our senior team’s experience in the industry stretches back more than twenty years, through every change in tax law, filing regime and software along the way.

Clients come to us for a tax return or a tidy-up, and stay for years. Our client reviews tell that story better than we can: 5.0 from 21 reviews, with reviewers describing relationships of five, ten and more years.

How we work

What “proactive” actually means here

Plenty of firms file what you send them. We think that is the smallest part of the job.

Ahead of deadlines

Every filing date, from VAT and payroll to accounts and self assessment, is diarised and hit early, so penalties simply never happen.

A person, not a portal

You get direct access to the people doing your work. Responsive, polite and efficient: their words, not ours.

Advice before the fact
